KNOW US
ESME is an abbreviation of "Egyptian Society of Materials Engineers". It is the first
scientific society oriented to metallurgical and
materials engineering students in Egypt and North Africa region.
ESME was founded in 2009 at the faculty of Petroleum and
Mining Engineering, Suez University.
After five years of evolution, ESME became a unique student
chapter and exceeded the limit of 70 active members and still
attracting more.
ESME Cairo is our first branch founded for both metallurgical and mechanical
engineering students at Cairo university.
ABOUT OUR CHAPTER
ESME is dedicated to qualifying "Materials Engineering Students" to suffice the job
market with professional fresh graduates who can Bridge the gap between real science
& technology opportunities in the fields of QA/QC engineering ,material engineering
,corrosion engineering ,welding ,steel making ,metal processing and industrial safety.
We take responsibility for building our members' character by both improving their soft
skills and enhancing their technical knowledge. 
Our belief in leadership skills for engineers is firm. Simply, leadership is the only way to
fulfill prosperous careers, so we build leaders and actuators who can change the reality
with their teams.

Recommended Content Direction
How to repeat the wins and push more views from upcoming posts.
1. Lead with people
Keep using real faces, names, titles, achievements, and team moments. This is the strongest
view driver.
2. Make the CTA early
For recruitment and committee posts, place the main action in the first line: apply, comment,
share, or message.
3. Turn posts into series
Create repeatable formats: Board Spotlight, Behind the Team, Committee Deep Dive, and
Student Opportunity.
4. Optimize for 18–24
Use direct captions, clean contrast, short hooks, and designs that feel like one consistent ESME
visual family.
Next reporting step
Track the same KPIs weekly: views, viewers, interactions, engagement rate, clicks, comments, and shares.
